Enablement Rule: Know When Not to Use AI

Good AI enablement includes knowing when to keep a task manual. Skip public AI tools when handling material non-public information (MNPI), client banking credentials, binding tax or legal opinions, or unverified financial projections. In a broker-dealer context this utility serves as a first-pass training aid, not a substitute for human compliance review.
