# Plain-Language AI Policy (Broker-Dealer Edition)

This policy explains how to use AI at work without leaking sensitive data, violating FINRA rules, or embarrassing the firm with sloppy writing.

---

## The Short Version
- **Use approved tools only** for work tasks - never personal or free accounts with company data.
- **Never paste private data** (client info, deal details, passwords) into any AI tool. Manually redact sensitive data. Always.
- **You own the output.** If AI hallucinates a number or writes something non-compliant, it is your mistake, not the model's. Double-check your work. Then check it again.
- **AI assists; people decide.** Never let AI make compliance, financial, or personnel decisions - or any decisions at all. You own the work, and you stay in control. It is a tool, not an authority. It does what you say, not the other way around.

---

## 1. Approved vs. Unapproved Tools
- **Approved Tools:** Use only the company-vetted AI tools listed in our internal knowledge base. These accounts have enterprise privacy settings turned on so our data is not used to train public models.
- **Unapproved / Personal Tools:** Do not use personal ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or random browser extensions for company work - even if you are just "trying to clean up an email."
- **Want a new tool?** Submit a quick request to IT and Compliance before testing it with any real work data.

---

## 2. What Data You Can (and Cannot) Paste

**RED - Never Paste Into Any AI Tool:**
- Client Personally Identifiable Information (PII): names paired with SSNs, account numbers, tax IDs, addresses, or bank details.
- Material Non-Public Information (MNPI) or confidential deal terms.
- Passwords, API keys, private credentials, or internal security configurations.
- HR records, performance reviews, or employee medical/personal details.

**YELLOW - Approved Enterprise Tools Only (and Redact First):**
- Internal memos, meeting notes, and draft strategies (strip out specific client names and dollar figures whenever possible).
- Anonymized data sets for formatting, summarizing, or brainstorming.

**GREEN - Safe to Use in Approved Tools:**
- Publicly available information (published articles, public SEC/FINRA rules, general market news).
- Generic templates, code snippets, formatting help, and blank-slate brainstorming.

---

## 3. Client-Facing Work & FINRA Rule 2210
Everything we send to clients or the public must be fair, balanced, transparent, and not misleading. AI tends toward exaggeration and sycophancy, so you must actively scrub its drafts for:
- **Promissory language or guarantees:** Cut phrases like *"guaranteed returns," "risk-free," "proven to outperform,"* or *"will maximize your yield."*
- **One-sided claims:** If a draft highlights benefits without clearly stating the risks or limitations, rewrite it.
- **Fabricated citations or stats:** Never trust a statistic, tax rule, or legal citation generated by an LLM. Verify it against the primary source yourself.
